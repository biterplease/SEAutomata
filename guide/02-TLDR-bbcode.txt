[h3]Blocks:[/h3]

[list]
[*][b]Drone controller[/b]: does all the work. Besides the expected tools of any drone, it needs
[list]
[*][b]A camera[/b]: to detect obstacles in its flight path
[*][b]A sensor[/b]: to detect obstacles nearby
[*][b]An antenna[/b]: to communicate with the network. Range can be small (20m-50m), as long as it can reach its "boss" the orchestrator.
[/list]
[*][b]Orchestrator[/b]: coordinates all the work.
[*][b]Logistics Computer[/b]: tells the orchestrator what is available. Needed for inventory access.
[*][b]Construction Computer[/b]: tells the orchestrator what needs to be built or ground.
[*][b]Mining Surveyor[/b]: collects ore survey data and tells the orchestrator what needs to be mined.
[*][b]Target designator[/b]: grid version of the handheld Industrial/Hostile Target Designator, with 15km range.
[/list]

Not blocks, but the handhelds [b]Industrial Target Designator[/b] and [b]Hostile Target Designator[/b] do the same job as the Target Designator block, with a 5km range.

[h3]The network:[/h3]

[list]
[*]To form a network, antennaes need to be within range of each other. Laser antennas work too!
[*]Blocks on the same grid need no antenna.
[*]Docked drones talk to the grid they are connected to.
[*]Grid must be owned by you or your faction.
[/list]

[h3]To weld:[/h3]

[olist]
[*]Build a basic welder drone, add a Drone Controller. Set behavior profile to [i]Welder:All[/i].
[*]Set a drone's construction field through its home connector and GPS.
[*]You can visualize the area in the terminal or using [i]/automata debugdraw dronenav[/i].
[*]Place a block or project in the area, drone will build it, if you have the materials.
[/olist]

[h3]To weld, bigtime[/h3]

[olist]
[*]Build several basic welder drones, add a Drone Controller to each. Set behavior profiles to [i]Welder:All[/i].
[*]Place an Antenna, Construction Computer, Logistics Computer, and Orchestrator on a nearby grid.
[*]Use the Construction Computer terminal to resize, offset, or visualize its Construction Field.
[*]Place a block or project in the area, the drones will build it.
[/olist]

[h3]To grind[/h3]

[olist]
[*]See [b]Weld[/b] sections, replace the welders by grinders. Set behavior profile to [i]Grinder[/i]
[*]Select the grind color of the Drone Controller or Construction Computer.
[*]Paint things that color, they will be ground.
[/olist]

[h3]To survey for ores[/h3]

[olist]
[*]Place an ore detector on a drone
[*]Place an Antenna, Orchestrator, and a Mining Surveyor on a nearby grid.
[*]Make an Industrial Target Designator at any SKit or assembler.
[*]Point your Industrial Target Designator in TAG mode to the drone.
[*]Point your Industrial Target Designator in SPOT mode to where you suspect the ores are located
[*]Your drone will fly there, and gather data. Once the drone is back, it will report to the Mining Surveyor what is found.
[/olist]

[h3]To survey for ores, bigtime[/h3]

[olist]
[*]Place an ore detector on several drones. Set their behavior profiles to [i]Ore Scout[/i]
[*]Place an Antenna, Orchestrator, and a Mining Surveyor on a nearby grid.
[*]Make an Industrial Target Designator at any SKit or assembler.
[*]Point your Industrial Target Designator in TAG mode to the drones.
[*]Point your Industrial Target Designator in AREA mode to where you suspect the ores are located.
[*]Your drones will fly there, and perform an area sweep while gathering data. Once the drones are back, they will report to the Mining Surveyor what has been found.
[/olist]

[b]NOTES[/b]:

[list]
[*]Only drones with the "ore scout" behavior profile can perform Area Sweeps.
[*]Area sweep on a planet: covers a base area of 100m radius, for each drone added, up to 8, the radius increases 75m (to a max of 625m)
[*]Area sweep on an asteroid: paths are drawn along the surface of asteroid, and drones follow it. 
[/list]

[h3]To mine[/h3]

[olist]
[*]Build a mining drone that can bore, meaning that its hull can fit the hole the drills make.
[*]Place an Antenna, Orchestrator, Logistics Computer and a Mining Surveyor on a nearby grid.
[*]Survey for ores, the Mining Surveyor needs data to place an order.
[*]Use the Mining Surveyor terminal to create an ore order.
[/olist]

[b]NOTES[/b]:

[list]
[*]Mining orders exhaust themselves as the ore comes in. There is an option to make them permanent, but ore and stone will likely overflow your cargo.
[*]Stone is mined, not dumped. Due to this, orders are a bit slow, but after a few trips your ores show up.
[*]You are free to deal with it yourself through any method you like.
[*]Logistics Computer only accept ores if the grid its on has a refinery, or ice if it has an O2/H2 gen. You need to tick "Accept ores/ice" in order to take them without these blocks.
[/list]

[h3]To request items:[/h3]

[olist]
[*]Place an Antenna, Orchestrator, and Logistics Computer on the grid that wants items.
[*]Place an Antenna, Orchestrator, and Logistics Computer on the grid that has items.
[*]Ensure the grids are either within antenna range, or connected via laser antennas
[*]Check the [i]Request[/i] work mode on the grid that wants items
[*]Check the [i]Provide for logistics[/i] work mode on the grid that wants items.
[*]On the grid that wants items' Logistics Computer, configure the [i]ITEM:MIN:MAX[/i] you want
[*]A drone will pick up the items at one grid, and deliver to the other grid.
[/olist]

[h3]To fight:[/h3]

[olist]
[*]Create a combat drone, place an AI Flight, AI Defensive, AI Offensive, and a Drone Controller on it
[*]Configure the "Combat handover" section of the drone
[*]If an enemy approaches within the defensive drone's range, your drone will engage it.
[*]You can use the Hostile Target Designator to TAG drones and then use ENGAGE mode to send them to attack a target.
[*]Use RECALL mode to send them back.
[/olist]

[b]NOTES[/b]:

[list]
[*]Its the AI Flight, AI Defensive, and AI Offensive doing the fighting, make sure they're configured beforehand
[*]The Drone Controller only handles takeoff, and travel to target. Once no more targets are detected, the Drone Controller takes over again and returns home.
[/list]

[h3]To protect:[/h3]

[olist]
[*]The Hostile Target Designator can tag drones in TAG mode.
[*]Point the Hostile Target Designator at another grid in ESCORT mode, the tagged combat drones will follow and defend it.
[*]If you point the Hostile Target Designator at the ground near yourself, and fire in ESCORT mode, the tagged drones will follow and defend you instead.
[*]After engaging, they will return to escorting.
[*]Use RECALL mode to send them back.
[/olist]

[h3]Debug drawing commands[/h3]

All debug draw commands time out after a short time (as defined in the .ini). Each argument draws a specific aspect of a specific block Usage:

[code]
/automata debugdraw [dronenav] [droneobs] [ccobs] [park] [lanes] [off] [range]
[/code]

Where:

[list]
[*][i]dronenav[/i] drone navigation path and waypoints
[*][i]droneobs[/i] drone's construction field
[*][i]ccobs[/i] construction computer field
[*][i]park[/i] parking field defined by the Orchestrator
[*][i]lanes[/i] navigation lanes defined by the orchestrator
[*][i]range[/i] Automata entities within this range will accept the draw order. 0-2000 meters, default 300
[*][i]off[/i] turns off all debug drawing, regardless of range
[/list]
